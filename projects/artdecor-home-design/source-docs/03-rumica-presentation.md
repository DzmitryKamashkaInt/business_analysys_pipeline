## Positioning
"The main thing is not to own — the main thing is to connect" (like Airbnb/Uber/Amazon models). Rumica connects people dreaming of a beautiful interior with the design and products to realize it. 85% of buying behavior is driven by anticipation of the future outcome.

## Platform Description
Rumica — innovative web platform for interior planning and visualization.
- Create interior designs integrating real products from a furniture/decor catalog; buy directly through the platform.
- Upload own floor plans or choose catalog templates; apply real furniture/finish items.
- Save, export, and share projects; "public designs" feature builds a community where users inspire and adapt each other's work.
- **For B2B audience**: new promotion/sales channels + user-behavior analytics.
- **For B2C audience**: intuitive planning tools, access to real products, professional-level visualization.
- Tech basis: interactive 2D planner (Three.js) with Bitrix24 integration. Future versions: 3D visualizer + AI module.

## Prototype Walkthrough (as shown in mockups)
Three steps ("From idea to reality"):
1. **Planning** — upload or draw room layout, set dimensions, add windows/doors.
2. **Furniture** — choose interior items from catalog, place them in room zones, experiment with real-scale arrangement.
3. **Visualization** — adjust lighting/angles to preview realistic look.
- Inspiration gallery: ready-made design styles (Scandinavian, minimalism, French chic, etc.).
- CTA: "Create your first project today."

### Catalog page mockup
- Unique selling point: closes the loop from visualization to purchase — user sees furniture chosen in the designer and can buy it immediately.
- Filters: by price (budget to premium), by category (sofas, tables, cabinets, decor, etc.), by room (living room, kitchen, bedroom, dining room, etc.).

### AI Assistant mockup (notably NOT in the original Intetics MVP scope — shown here as if already in early prototype)
- Natural-language design instructions, e.g.: "Move the table to the center," "Make the wall lighter," "Bed in the middle," "Tile floor."
- Benefits claimed: accessibility (no design knowledge needed), speed (fast changes), personalization (AI learns preferences).
- **Flag**: The Intetics proposal explicitly scopes AI recommendations as Post-MVP. This presentation shows an AI assistant as part of the (apparently already-built or mocked) prototype UI — a potential **scope discrepancy/unconfirmed addition** to reconcile with the client.

## Market Analysis
- Global interior-design software market (Grand View Research): $5.4B (2024) → $9.7B (2030), CAGR 10.3% (2025–2030).
- Growth drivers: demand for comfortable/aesthetic/functional spaces, remote work, urbanization, lifestyle changes.
- Russia + Belarus (key target markets) market size 2024: **$103–195M** (ASER estimate); low competition/supply in this region despite global market growth — seen as a market opportunity.

Rumica's stated differentiators:

## Target Audience & Value
**Target segments:**
- B2C private users: homeowners/new movers, renters, design enthusiasts, secondary-housing owners.
- Design professionals: designers, architects, renovation companies, furniture/decor manufacturers.
- Developers/homebuilders (застройщики): process optimization and client acquisition.
- Educational institutions: students and instructors.

**Value delivered:**
- Detailed digital models of spaces before physical realization.
- Experiment with color/materials/furniture/decor at no extra cost.
- Minimize errors/rework in eventual physical implementation.
- Accelerate concept development and decision-making.

## Marketing Strategy
Tools: content marketing (tutorials, videos, webinars), social media (Instagram, Pinterest), partnerships (design studios, influencers), free trial versions, customer review tracking, event presentations (conferences/exhibitions), SEO, email marketing, promotions/discounts/contests.

**Marketing budget: USD 120,000/quarter**, planned for 2027–2031 (split across B2B and B2C monetization channels).

## Monetization Model (multi-channel)
- Subscriptions & licenses — for designers/architects (B2B)
- Brand partnerships — connecting/promoting furniture brands (B2B)
- User subscriptions — Free/PRO model
- Direct sales — products and design packs
- Project marketplace — selling finished design solutions
- Advertising placements — banners and native ads
